The problem

Most practices don't have a billing problem. They have a follow-up problem.

Claims go out. Then nobody watches what comes back. Here's what that usually looks like by the time a practice calls us.

Insurance A/R keeps aging

Balances roll past 90 and 120 days because nobody is working the queue daily. By the time anyone looks, the appeal window has closed.

Denials aren't being worked

Rejections get filed instead of fixed. The same avoidable denial reason repeats month after month because nobody is tracking the pattern back to its source.

You can't get a straight answer

You ask where a claim stands and get a report instead of an answer. No named contact, no visibility, no sense that anyone is accountable for the number.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do we have to change our practice management software?

No. We work inside the system you already use — TherapyNotes, CollaborateMD, NextGen, Greenway or Tebra. There's no migration, no second licence to buy, and no new workflow for your front desk to learn.

How quickly can you take over?

A fully executed RCM takeover happens within 90 days of the kickoff call. We can move faster on some pieces, but we don't recommend rushing the setup phase — that's where the errors that cost you money six months later get built in.

What happens to our existing aged A/R?

We review it during discovery and tell you honestly what's recoverable and what isn't. Working legacy A/R is scoped in the proposal so you know exactly what's being pursued and on what terms.

Will we have a specific person to contact?

Yes. You get a named team that knows your practice and your payers, and you can reach them directly. That's the whole point — most practices come to us because they couldn't get anyone on the phone.

How is this priced?

Pricing is scoped to your practice after the discovery review, based on volume, specialty and which parts of the cycle we're taking on. We'll walk you through it in the proposal — no surprise line items.
